Here’s a concise summary of the ATC scheduling requirements and constraints based on the inputs provided:

### General Schedule:
- **Shifts**: Morning, Afternoon, Night, Night-off, Clear-off (rest day).
- **Weekly Cycle**: 5 days on, 2 days off; shift cycle follows sequence (e.g., morning → afternoon → night → night-off → clear-off).
- **Teams**: 5 teams (A, B, C, D, E) working on a rotating cycle, with balanced distribution of rated controllers across teams.

### Controller Attributes:
- **Ratings**: Seven different levels (e.g., non-rated, trainee).
- **Medical** and **English Language Proficiency**: Validity based on start and end dates.
- **Leave Applications**: Currently approved manually; track status (approved, pending).

### Staffing Requirements:
- **Stations**: Vary in the minimum required controllers per shift, often using a 3/2 multiplier (e.g., 3 assigned for 2 required).
- **Shift Requirements**: Minimum manpower varies by station and shift.

### Duty and Rest Constraints:
- **Continuous Work**: Max 12 hours per shift.
- **Weekly Limit**: 48 hours.
- **Monthly Limit**: 90 hours over 30 days.
- **Continuous Days**: Max 6 days on duty; requires a 48-hour break afterward.
- **Night Shifts**: Require 48-hour break after night duty; 56 hours if two consecutive night shifts.
- **Morning Shift**: Requires 12-hour gap following completion.

### Shift Allocation:
- **Shift In-Charge**: Assigns controllers to specific positions, ensuring:
  - No more than 2 hours on a single channel (typically 1.5 hours followed by 45-minute break).
  - Proper ratings and currency for each position.
  - Proficiency requirement: work 10 hours within the last 6 months over any 6-day period.

### Roster Requirements:
- **Roster Cycle**: Planned bi-weekly (fortnightly) and published at least 5 days in advance.

### Rewards:
- **Desirable**: Equal distribution of work hours, adherence to 5-day cycle.
- **Non-desirable**: Extra hours, failure to ensure required breaks after night duty, consecutive night shifts.
